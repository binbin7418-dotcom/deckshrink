## One-liner

Your 40 MB deck is 95% photos. DeckShrink compresses the pictures embedded inside your .pptx, .docx and .xlsx files, writes slim new copies — and re-opens every output to prove it still works.

## The problem

A client deck, a training manual, a report workbook — they balloon because every pasted phone photo is stored at full 4000×3000 resolution forever. Email rejects them, SharePoint crawls, and "compress pictures" inside Office is one file at a time, one sitting at a time, with no proof afterwards that nothing broke.

## What it does

- Batch: drop in files or a whole folder of .pptx / .docx / .xlsx
- Re-encodes each embedded picture at a sane cap (1280–2560 px, your choice) and JPEG quality — **format unchanged**: a PNG stays a PNG, a JPEG stays a JPEG, so nothing in your file changes type
- Optionally removes the package thumbnail part *and* its relationship entry (the leftover most tools forget)
- Writes NEW files (`name_slim.pptx`) — originals are never modified
- **Re-check layer:** every output must pass three proofs — zip integrity test, every picture re-decoded, and every non-picture part compared **byte-for-byte** against the original. The slimming report shows per-file before/after sizes and the verdict
- Honest accounting: if a file was already well optimized and doesn't shrink, the report says so instead of inventing a saving

## Measured (seeded acceptance test, 11/11 checks passed)

- Seeded 18.4 MB deck (4000×3000 JPEG + 3000×2000 PNG + thumbnail) → **4.6 MB out (75% saved)**, both pictures slimmed and capped at 1920 px, formats unchanged
- Thumbnail part *and* its package relationship both removed
- Non-picture parts (slides, content types, core props) byte-identical after slimming; original file untouched (hash-verified)

## Honest limits

- Only pictures *embedded in Office files* — standalone image files are our ImageBatch tool's job, and PDFs are out of scope
- Macro files (.pptm/.docm/.xlsm) are skipped in this version
- Animated GIFs and BMPs inside files are left as-is rather than risked
